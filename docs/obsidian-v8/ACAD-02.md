# ACAD-02 — Mandatory Pairs

Section: [[Section 4 - Document-Specific Rules]]
Subsection: 4.8 ACADEMIC CERTIFICATES

every level needs both its certificate and its transcript. Missing transcript or certificate -> FAIL, except FLAG while a document whose level couldn't be read might be the missing partner. An apostille not followed by an academic document -> FLAG. No academic documents at all -> FLAG. *(approved)*
