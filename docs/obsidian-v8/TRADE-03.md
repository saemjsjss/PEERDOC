# TRADE-03 — Validity

Section: [[Section 4 - Document-Specific Rules]]
Subsection: 4.6 TRADE LICENSE

the fiscal year runs 1 July - 30 June. On the check date the licence PASSes if it is valid until at least the 30 June that ends the current fiscal year (from a "Valid up to" date, or the fiscal year printed, e.g. 2026-2027 = valid until 30-06-2027), OR it was issued on or after the 1 July that started it. Either one is enough. Checked in January 2027: valid until 30-06-2027, or issued since 01-07-2026. Neither -> FAIL, including when no such date is found. A date that would pass but was read with low confidence -> FLAG. A renewal page listing several years counts the latest. *(approved)*
