# StudentRef

Section: [[Section 2 - Identity Anchors & Assignment (Pass 2)]]
Subsection: 2.1 The Anchors:

from the Passport; MRZ name and printed name must match. Digits in the MRZ name are read as their look-alike letters (0->O, 1->I, 2->Z, 5->S, 6->G, 8->B), since names hold no digits. A reliably read MRZ name that matches confirms a printed name read with low confidence.

## Notes
- Birth Certificates are the student's only. Parents are identified by NID or passport, never by their own birth certificate.
