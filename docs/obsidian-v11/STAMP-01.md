# STAMP-01 — Date

Section: [[Section 4 - Document-Specific Rules]]
Subsection: 4.12 NOTARY AND TRANSLATION DATES

on every document, each notary date (on or beside the notary's stamp: "09 JUN 2026", "Date: 23/06/2026") and each translator date (beside "Translated by") must be within 3 months. Older -> FAIL. A date read below 0.85 confidence -> FLAG. Translation and notarisation may be on different days. Document dates printed near the notary block (publication date, issue date, a birth date in a table) are not notary dates.
