# DEATH-05 — Consistency

Section: [[Section 4 - Document-Specific Rules]]
Subsection: 4.11 DEATH CERTIFICATE (replaces a parent's NID)

no financial document may present the deceased parent as a living sponsor. An Employment Certificate for them issued after the date of death -> FAIL. Any other Employment Certificate, Trade License or TIN in their name -> FLAG.
