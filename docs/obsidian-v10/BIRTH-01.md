# BIRTH-01 — Translation

Section: [[Section 4 - Document-Specific Rules]]
Subsection: 4.3 BIRTH CERTIFICATE (the student's only)

needs an English translation (Section 0). A bilingual certificate (Bangla and English on one page, e.g. BDRIS) needs no translation page when its English side gives the child's name, date of birth, father's name and mother's name; any of them not reliable -> FLAG. *(approved)*
