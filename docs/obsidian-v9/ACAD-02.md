# ACAD-02 — Mandatory Pairs

Section: [[Section 4 - Document-Specific Rules]]
Subsection: 4.8 ACADEMIC CERTIFICATES (SSC / HSC: secondary / higher secondary school certificates;

every level needs both its certificate and its transcript. One missing -> FAIL, but FLAG while a document whose level is not reliable might be the missing one. An apostille not followed by an academic document -> FLAG. No academic documents at all -> FLAG. *(approved)*
