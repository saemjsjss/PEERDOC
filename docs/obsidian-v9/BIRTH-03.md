# BIRTH-03 — DOB

Section: [[Section 4 - Document-Specific Rules]]
Subsection: 4.3 BIRTH CERTIFICATE (the student's only)

compared with the Passport date of birth. A Bangla certificate with an English translation: the year only. An English certificate (old BDR Form-3, bilingual BDRIS): the full date. A different year FAILs even when the dates are days apart (31-12-2001 vs 01-01-2002). A low-confidence date PASSes only if it equals the passport date exactly; otherwise -> FLAG.
