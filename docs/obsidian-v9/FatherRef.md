Section: [[Section 2 - Identity Anchors & Assignment (Pass 2)]]
Subsection: 2.1 The Anchors:

the father's name on the student's Birth Certificate. Only when no Birth Certificate has that field at all is the Passport's Father's Name used (an unreliable Birth Certificate value is FLAG, not a reason to switch).

## Notes
- Birth Certificates are the student's only. A parent is identified by their NID or Death Certificate, never by their own birth certificate.
